Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our services

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings, we recommend booking 6-12 months in advance to ensure availability. For other sessions, 2-4 weeks notice is typically sufficient.

How long until I receive my photos?

Portrait sessions are typically delivered within 2 weeks. Wedding galleries take 4-6 weeks for full editing and delivery.

Do you travel for destination weddings?

Absolutely! We love destination weddings. Travel fees apply based on location. Contact us for a custom quote.

What is your payment policy?

We require a 30% non-refundable retainer to secure your date, with the remaining balance due 2 weeks before your session or event.

Can I request specific edits or styles?

Yes! During our consultation, we discuss your preferred editing style. We also offer additional retouching services upon request.
